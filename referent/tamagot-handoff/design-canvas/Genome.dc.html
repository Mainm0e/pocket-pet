GENOME SPEC · V1

How a seed becomes a tamagot

12 million possible gene mixes
18 billion with names
01 · SEED Any text Your name, a birthday, “MOCHI-001”. Trimmed and uppercased, so “mochi” and “MOCHI” match.
02 · HASH One number FNV-1a squeezes the text into a 32-bit number. Change one letter and the number changes completely.
03 · ROLL A fixed dice stream That number starts a mulberry32 random stream. Same seed, same rolls, on every device.
04 · EXPRESS 10 genes, 20 bases Each gene rolls an allele by its odds, then a bonus roll may add a unique part. Every result becomes a two-letter codon from A, T, G and C.
05 · GRADE Rarity tier Rare alleles add points. The total sets the tier, from Common up to Legendary.

IDEA · MAKE IT YOURS Owner seed Hatch from your handle plus the moment you pressed the egg: “MAIN·2026-10-18·06:02”. Nobody else can land the same pet unless you share the seed.
IDEA · BREEDING Two parents, one egg The child’s seed is “A×B”. Its own stream flips a coin per gene to take Mom’s or Dad’s codon, and 1 gene in 50 mutates to a fresh roll.
IDEA · SHARE CODE DNA as a pet card The 18-base strand is the pet’s fingerprint. Print it on a trading card or QR code, and anyone can rebuild the exact tamagot from it.
